## SLIDE 4: How It Works

**For Creators:**

**For Restaurants:**

## SLIDE 5: HungerScore™ — The Moat

**Proprietary Ranking System**

HungerScore™ evaluates every photo on:
- **Conversion potential**: Will this swipe-right + actually order?
- **Visual appeal**: Lighting, composition, food styling
- **Completeness**: Menu match, pricing visible, dietary tags present
- **Viral coefficient**: Shares, saves, pattern of high engagement

Only high-HungerScore photos get featured. This means:
- Eaters see only the most appetizing, order-ready content
- Restaurants get distribution tied to proven conversion
- Creators are incentivized to shoot higher-quality, more effective photos

**HungerScore is the recommendation engine that makes the whole flywheel spin.**

## SLIDE 6: Revenue Model

**Three Revenue Streams**

| Stream | Description | Take Rate |
|--------|-------------|-----------|
| **Creator Commissions** | 10–20% of the commission creators set | Platform keeps 20% of commission |
| **Restaurant Listings** | Featured placement, verified badge | $99–499/month |
| **Sponsored Photos** | Brands pay to appear in feeds | $5–50 CPM |

**Unit Economics:**
- Avg order: $25
- Avg commission: 12%
- Platform take: 20% of commission = $0.60 per order
- At 100K monthly orders: **$60,000/month recurring**

**Creator Example:**
- Photographer uploads 50 photos
- 10 photos drive 5 orders/month each = 50 orders/month
- At $25/order × 12% commission × 80% to platform = **$120/month passive**
