## Executive Summary

HungerSwipes is a two-sided marketplace that connects food lovers with local food vendors through a Tinder-style swiping interface. Creators upload food photos, earn commission on every order driven through their content, and build a passive income stream. Eaters discover new food, save matches, and unlock exclusive discounts.

**The hook:** Your food photos pay you forever — not just when you post, but every single time someone orders from your recommendation.

**Market size:** US food delivery market = $200B+ annually. Creator economy = $480B by 2027. Food photography = untapped niche.

---

## The Problem

### For Eaters
- Food apps show menus. HungerSwipes shows **what people actually order and love.**
- Discovery is broken — people eat the same 5 restaurants on rotation
- No rewards for being a food trendsetter

### For Creators (Food Photographers, Influencers, Regular People)
- Instagram/TikTok reach is declining; brands don't pay fairly
- No passive income from old content
- Platform algorithm decides what sticks, not the food

### For Vendors (Restaurants, Food Trucks, Ghost Kitchens)
- Expensive commissions from DoorDash/Grubhub (30-40%)
- HungerSwipes charges **10-15%** — 3-4x cheaper
- Food photos are proven marketing; HungerSwipes provides them for free
- Pay only when HungerSwipes actually drives a customer

---

## The Solution

**HungerSwipes** = Tinder + Uber Eats + Creator Economy

- **Eaters** swipe through beautiful food photos, save matches, order directly
- **Creators** upload food photos, set commission rate (5-20%), earn on every order
- **Vendors** get cheap, high-quality marketing with performance pricing only

## Business Model

### Revenue Streams

| Stream | Description | Take Rate |
|--------|-------------|-----------|
| **Order Commission** | 10% of every order placed through a matched photo | 10% of order value |
| **Creator Tiers** | Free (5% max commission), Pro $9.99/mo (15% max), Elite $29.99/mo (20% max) | Monthly subscription |
| **Vendor Subscriptions** | Featured placement, promoted discount codes | $49-$199/mo |
| **Tip Processing** | 3% payment processing on tips | 3% of tips |
| **Future: Marketplace** | P2P food photo licensing | 15% of license fee |

### Platform Fee Structure
- **Seller (vendor) payout:** 67%
- **Creator commission:** 5-20% (set by creator, locked at order time)
- **Platform:** 10% + Stripe fees (~3%)

### Unit Economics — Per Order ($25 average)

```
Eater pays:                  $25.00
Stripe processing (3%):      -$0.75
                               ------
Net to platform:             $24.25

Vendor receives (67%):       $16.25
Creator earns (15% avg):     $3.64
Platform keeps (10%):        $2.43
Payment processor (3%):      $0.75
                               ------
                               $25.00 ✓
```

## Go-to-Market Strategy

### Phase 1 — MVP Launch (Month 1-2)
**Goal: 100 creators, 50 vendors, $10K GMV/month**

1. **Founders/beta creators** — seed with 20 food photographers in St. Louis + New Orleans
2. **Restaurant partnerships** — approach 50 local restaurants, offer free featured listing for first 3 months
3. **Social launch** — O D Porter posts across personal channels (Instagram 1K+, TikTok, etc.)
4. **Food truck events** — partner with 5 food trucks, cover events with photo content

### Phase 2 — City Expansion (Month 3-6)
**Goal: 1,000 creators, 500 vendors, $100K GMV/month**

1. **University cities first** — students are creators AND eaters (cheap food discovery)
2. **Creator referral program** — give creators 3% of every order their referrals make
3. **Instagram/TikTok growth** — post daily food content, hashtag strategy
4. **Vendor case studies** — "how X restaurant got 40 new customers from one food photo"

### Phase 3 — National (Month 7-18)
**Goal: 10K creators, 5K vendors, $1M GMV/month**

1. **Micro-influencer outreach** — 1K-10K follower food accounts
2. **Restaurant association partnerships** — B2B sales team
3. **Creator fund** — 20% of platform revenue goes to top creators monthly
4. **City ambassador program** — local rep in each city (commission-based)

### Phase 4 — Scale (Year 2+)
**Goal: 100K creators, 25K vendors, $10M GMV/month**

1. **Ghost kitchen network** — exclusive menu drops
2. **Brand partnerships** — CPG brands sponsor food photo challenges
3. **White-label** — license the platform to food media companies
4. **International** — Canada, UK, Australia

## Competitive Landscape

| Platform | Commission | Audience | Creator Income | Swipe Model |
|----------|-----------|----------|----------------|-------------|
| **HungerSwipes** | **10%** | Food-focused | **Per-order commission** | ✅ Yes |
| DoorDash | 30-40% | Everyone | None | ❌ |
| Uber Eats | 30-40% | Everyone | None | ❌ |
| Instagram | 0% | Everyone | Brand deals (rare) | ❌ |
| TikTok | 0% | Everyone | Creator fund (pennies) | ❌ |
| Yelp | 0% | Reviews | None | ❌ |
| Too Good To Go | 0% | Discount surplus | None | ❌ |

**HungerSwipes wins because:**
1. It's the ONLY platform that pays creators per order, forever
2. It's the CHEAPEST channel for vendors (10% vs 30-40%)
3. It's the MOST FUN way to discover food (swipe vs scroll menus)
