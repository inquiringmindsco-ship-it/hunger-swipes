# HungerSwipes — Marketing Plan
**April 2026**

---

## Brand Positioning

**Tagline:** "Swipe. Order. Earn forever."

**Positioning statement:**
HungerSwipes is the only platform where your food photos pay you a commission — forever — every time someone orders from your recommendation.

**Brand voice:** Bold, hungry, a little irreverent. We're for food lovers who know what's good and aren't shy about sharing it.

**Target audience:**
- Primary: Food lovers 18-35 who document their meals (Instagram/TikTok native)
- Secondary: Restaurant owners and food vendors looking for cheap marketing
- Tertiary: Micro-influencers (1K-100K followers) looking for passive income

## Creator Acquisition Strategy

### Why creators will join
- **Passive income:** Upload once, earn every order — not just brand deals
- **It's free:** No cost to sign up, upload photos, earn commission
- **Easy:** Takes 2 minutes to upload a photo
- **Proven:** HungerSwipes shows them exactly how much they've earned

### Creator Acquisition Channels

| Channel | Tactic | Expected CAC |
|---------|--------|-------------|
| Instagram DM | Cold DM food accounts (under 10K) | $0-5 |
| TikTok comments | Comment on popular food videos | $0-2 |
| Reddit | r/FoodPhotography, r/Restaurant, r/Entrepreneur | $0 |
| Email outreach | food bloggers under 5K followers | $5-10 |
| Referral program | Existing creators refer others | $15 |
| Creator marketplaces | NinjaOutreach, Collabstr | $25-50 |

### Cold DM Script (Instagram)

```
Hey [NAME]! 👋

I noticed you post amazing food photos — I run a food app called HungerSwipes.

Creators on there earn a commission every time someone orders from their food photo. One creator made $340 last month from photos she posted 6 months ago.

We're launching in [CITY] soon and I'd love to have you as a founding creator. Free forever + early access.

Interested?
```

### Creator Referral Program

- Refer a creator → get **$5 credit** when they earn their first $25
- Refer 5 creators → get **Pro tier free** for 3 months
- Top referrer each month → **$100 bonus**

## Referral Programs

### Eater Referral (Bounty Model)
- Share your unique link → friend signs up + makes first order
- Both get **$5 credit** toward next order
- Cost: $10 total ( CAC = $10, high LTV of $50+)

### Creator Referral
- Share your unique link → friend signs up as creator + earns $25
- Referrer earns **$5 cash** + Pro tier upgrade
- Cost: $5 + tier discount, earns when referred creator is active

### Vendor Referral
- Share your unique link → restaurant signs up + processes first order
- Referrer earns **$25 credit** toward their next order
- Cost: $25, vendor LTV = $600+/year

## Week-By-Week Launch Plan

### Pre-Launch (Weeks -1 to 0)
- Finalize brand assets, copy, CTAs
- Set up all social accounts
- Seed database with 20 creators, 10 vendors, 100 mock photos
- Write launch content (5 TikToks, 3 blog posts)
- Set up analytics

### Launch Week (Week 1)
- Day 1: Soft launch to 50 people (email)
- Day 2: Post TikTok #1 (announcement)
- Day 3: DM 25 creators
- Day 4: Post TikTok #2 (demo)
- Day 5: Email entire waitlist
- Day 6: Post TikTok #3 (vendor story)
- Day 7: Analyze, fix bugs, DM follow-ups

### Post-Launch (Weeks 2-4)
- Daily: Post to TikTok + Instagram
- Weekly: DM 50 creators, email 25 vendors
- Track: Creator signup rate, vendor signup rate, GMV/day
- Fix: Any UX issues from first cohort

### Scale (Months 2-3)
- Activate referral programs
- Start paid ads ($500/month budget)
- Begin PR outreach (local media)
- Onboard city ambassadors (STL + NOLA)
- Collect testimonials and case studies
